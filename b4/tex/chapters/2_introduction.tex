\section{Introduction}

Photodiodes play a prominent role in sensor technology, as they are used in
various kinds of optical measurement devices. Incoming radiation creates electron-hole
pairs within the diode, which get separated in the diodes's space charge region. This
results in a measurable photocurrent. Optical power measurement devices, which are
building blocks for optical fiber receiver, CD players and smoke detectors %TODO This sounds like an arbitrary list (and a bit unscientific), can you rephrase that?
rely on photodiodes. Photodiodes are also utilized in spectroscopy, as it is possible
to utilize resolve the photon flux spatially using photodiode-arrays.

responsivity spectrum and photocurrent linearity for five distinct photodiodes
