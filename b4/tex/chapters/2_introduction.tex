\section{Introduction}

Photodiodes play a prominent role in sensor technology, as they are used in
various kinds of optical measurement devices. Incoming radiation creates electron-hole
pairs within the diode, which get separated in the diodes's space charge region.
This results in a measurable photocurrent. Optical power measurement devices,
which are building blocks for optical fiber receiver, CD players and smoke detectors
rely on photodiodes. Photodiodes are also utilized in spectroscopy, as they can resolve
photon flux spatially using photodiode-arrays.

Due to their technological impact, the laboratory procotol examines the
operating principle of a Schottky photodiode in chapters
\cref{sec:theory_basics} and \cref{sec:schottky}. Key metrics, such as the
responsivity spectrum and photocurrent linearity are examined in
\cref{sec:results} using the measurement methods in \cref{sec:methods}.
